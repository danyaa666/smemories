# E09_T-175 — Template gallery and apply, per section (web): shipped and own templates, preview, apply, delete own

**Epic:** E09-customisation-editor · **Milestone:** M4 · **Risk:** low (web only; the server side is T-172/T-173 and is the safety net) · **Priority:** P2 · **Type:** feature
**Depends on:** T-170 (page flow and toolbar rows merged), T-173 (`apply-template`, `template-create|delete`, `template-list` with `source` merged and approved by the owner). **Read first:** `docs/api-contract.md` (book-layout section as updated by T-172/T-173), `docs/templates.md` (page templates), ADR 0005 (`docs/adr/0005-doory-templates-and-fabric-studio.md`, Q3 and Q8), `web/src/book/editor/HistoryPanel.tsx`, `versions.ts`, `EditorShell.tsx` (how the History panel opens, previews a layout read-only, `store.settle()`, adopts an answer and clears undo, toast and live region), `useLayoutEditor.ts`, `store.ts`, `Stage.tsx`, `web/src/book/layout/runtime.tsx` (how a layout is drawn read-only), spec 24 (T-165: the preview/restore state machine is the pattern to copy), spec 29 (T-170: page strip, position text, notes-shared text).

#### Why
Owner request D-45: "user can select a template to apply for each page", per section: cover, profile, notes, back. The server can now list templates and apply one in a single transaction with one history snapshot; the student has no screen for it.

#### Requirements (SHALL)
1. **Entry point**: toolbar button "Templates" (vi "Mẫu trang", en "Templates"; icon + text) opening a side panel (dialog on phone widths) like History; Esc closes it and returns focus to the button; opening it closes the History panel and the reverse (one side panel at a time). The panel is **bound to the kind of the page being edited** (cover, profile, notes, back): title "Templates for the cover" (vi/en for each kind), and when the student steps to another page with the panel open the list follows the new kind. For notes, a line says "A notes template applies to every notes page" (reuse the T-170 `editor.notesShared` wording or a sibling key).
2. **List**: `book-layout/template-list {yearbook_id, kind}` when the panel opens and when the kind changes (never on editor load); two groups, "Shipped" and "My templates" (own, `source: "own"`, newest first); each card has the thumbnail, the label (locale: `label_vi`/`label_en` for shipped, the student's name for own), the item count, and the buttons *Preview* and *Apply* (own cards also *Delete*). Loading, empty ("No templates for this page yet." per group), error-with-retry, offline states. The list is cached per kind while the panel is open; a *Refresh* action re-calls it. No polling.
3. **Thumbnails** are the template's `page` drawn by the **same page renderer** at card size (CSS scale, read-only, no handles), with the book's real data (names, photos of the template's own elements), never a server-made image. At most 12 thumbnails are drawn at once (render the ones in view; the rest show a neutral placeholder). Own templates have no images (stripped by the server): their thumbnails show text, stickers and shapes only, and the card says so ("images not included"). No new dependency.
4. **Preview**: selecting *Preview* shows the current layout with `pages.<kind>` **replaced by the template's page**, read-only in the stage (the same read-only mechanism as T-165), banner "Previewing the template '<name>' on the cover — your page is unchanged" with *Apply to the cover* and *Back to current*. Items whose asset the client knows is gone are ignored by the renderer as the reader does; a hint "N items will be left out" may be computed from the asset registry (a hint only; the server's `dropped` is the truth). While previewing, the gesture and keyboard handlers of the editor are inactive; the page strip works only for the pages of the same kind (stepping to another kind leaves the preview and rebinds the panel). Keyboard: arrow keys move through the cards, Enter previews, Esc leaves the preview, then closes the panel.
5. **Apply**: *Apply* (from a card or from the preview banner) asks **one confirmation** naming the section and the consequence ("This replaces the layout of the cover: moved elements, added text, stickers and background. Your current version is kept in the history.") unless the page has no overrides, items or background (nothing to lose). On confirm: `store.settle()` first (flush in-flight and queued saves so the server's current version is the editor's), then `apply-template {yearbook_id, base_version: store version, kind, source, template_id}`. On success the editor **adopts** the answer (`layout`, `version`), clears the undo and redo stacks (the answer does not descend from the old history; the confirm text says so), leaves preview, closes the panel (focus to the Templates button), and announces "Template applied to the cover" plus, when `dropped > 0`, "N items could not be applied because their image or sticker no longer exists" in the live region and a visible toast with a link-like action *Undo in History* that opens the History panel on the new "Before template" row. A double click on *Apply* sends one request.
6. **History label**: `HistoryPanel`'s kind labels get the new snapshot kind `apply` ("Before template", vi "Trước khi áp dụng mẫu"); the T-165 unit test for labels is extended; an unknown future kind still renders a neutral label.
7. **Delete own**: *Delete* on an own card asks a confirm ("Delete the template 'X'? Books that already used it are not changed."), calls `template-delete {id}`, removes the card, announces it; a 404 (already gone) just refreshes the list. Delete is never offered on shipped cards.
8. **Errors** (vi and en, each with its own message; a failed apply leaves the editor, its history and its autosave exactly as before): `ERROR_VERSION_CONFLICT` (another tab saved: use the editor's existing conflict flow with the stored document; nothing is overwritten), `ERROR_INVALID_LAYOUT` (the template no longer fits; offer *Back to current* and *Refresh*), `ERROR_TEMPLATE_MISMATCH` (409: made on another design; refresh the list), 404 (template gone: refresh), rate limit/busy (retry-after), offline.
9. **Cost and guard rails**: no `template-list` call before the panel opens; list bodies are cached per kind for the panel's life, at most 2 kinds in memory; `apply-template` is the only write and it is never sent while a preview alone is open; autosave keeps running for pending edits only. The panel never mounts on a book without a layout block (templates without a `design.layout` have nothing to apply to: the button is hidden there, as the editor itself is).
10. **Accessibility and i18n**: the cards are a listbox/radio group with accessible names ("Template Clean title, 6 items, shipped"), the preview banner is a status region, the panel is in the editor's tab order, targets are at least 44 px on touch widths, all strings in vi and en (`lint:i18n` green).
11. **Tests**: unit tests for the template list model (grouping, ordering, labels by locale, kind switch, cache), the pure function that substitutes a page into a layout (`withPage(layout, kind, page)`: only that kind changes, `version` and other kinds untouched, the input is not mutated), the apply state machine with a fake API (settle first, `base_version` from the store, adopt, history cleared, every error mapped, double click once, dropped message, confirm skipped on an empty page), a component test for keyboard navigation; Playwright spec `tools/e2e/editor-templates.spec.mjs` on the real stack: open the editor, open Templates on the cover, preview then *Back to current* (page unchanged underneath), apply, reload and see it persisted, the History list shows the new "Before template" row, restore it and see the earlier cover, a notes template shows the "applies to every notes page" line and changes a second notes page too, two tabs applying at once give one conflict, delete an own template. If the stack has no shipped template for a kind, the spec first creates an own one through `book-layout/template-create` from the current page; it never depends on the content of shipped files.
12. **Docs**: `docs/editor-templates.md` (what the gallery does per section, preview vs apply, why undo is cleared and where "undo" lives, own vs shipped, limits, errors, manual check on a phone width); `docs/e2e.md` if commands change.

#### Acceptance criteria
- [ ] AC1 — A student opens Templates on the cover, previews a template (the stage shows it, the real page underneath is unchanged after *Back to current*), applies it, reloads and sees it; only the cover changed; the profile, notes and back pages are as before.
- [ ] AC2 — The replaced page is in History as "Before template"; restoring it brings the old cover back.
- [ ] AC3 — On a notes page the panel says the template applies to every notes page, and after applying every notes page shows it.
- [ ] AC4 — Every error of requirement 8 has its own vi/en message (unit-tested; conflict and delete also in Playwright); a failed apply changes nothing; a double click sends one request.
- [ ] AC5 — Nothing is fetched before the panel opens; at most 12 thumbnails are drawn at once; no new dependency.
- [ ] AC6 — Keyboard-only walk: open Templates, move through the cards, preview, apply (confirm), Esc returns focus to the Templates button; lint, prettier, tsc, vitest, `lint:i18n`, `check:api`, build and the e2e suite (incl. `editor-templates.spec.mjs`) green; nothing under `.team/`.
