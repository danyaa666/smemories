SMemories
design system

Light theme, mobile first. Colours, paper and tape come from your memory-book template, so the website and the printed book feel like one object. Tokens ship as CSS variables in tokens.css.

Decisions to check
!Fonts swapped for Vietnamese. Caveat, Karla and Jost have no Vietnamese glyphs. The site uses Fraunces + Be Vietnam Pro + Patrick Hand, and all three cover tiếng Việt.
✓Tomato is for the one main action on a screen. Teal is for links, focus and "approved".
✓Tape and rotation are only decoration. Forms and lists stay straight and calm.
