# Privacy Policy

**Version 2026-10-29 (draft).** This policy explains what SMemories stores, why, and what you can do about it.

## 1. Who is responsible
[operator name], [address], [contact email].

## 2. What we store
**If you have an account (owner):**
- your email address, the name you give, a username, your language;
- a password in hashed form (never the password itself), or the fact that you sign in with Google (your Google account id and email);
- your yearbooks: titles, school and class details, your profile text and photos, and your settings (template, page size).

**If you write a note through a link (friend):**
- what you type in the form (for example your name, your relationship to the owner and your message) and up to three photos.

**If you join a class (class member):**
- that you are a member of the class, your role in it (student or admin), when you joined, and which of your yearbooks you chose as your page in the class book;
- if you are a class admin, the invite links you make (only a scrambled form of the link is stored, with its expiry time).

**Technical data:**
- one sign-in cookie that keeps you signed in (no advertising or tracking cookies, no analytics);
- your network address, used only for a short time to limit abuse (for example too many sign-in attempts); it is not written to our logs;
- logs of requests (which page or action, the result and the time), kept for [N] days to keep the service running.

## 3. Why we use it
To run SMemories for you (accounts, yearbooks, notes, photos, print preview), to send the 6-digit codes we email you, and to protect the service against abuse. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Who sees it
- **Owners** see the notes and photos friends send to their yearbooks and decide which ones go into the book.
- **Friends** do not see each other's notes.
- **Class admins** (a teacher or class monitor) see the list of members of their class: names, role, when they joined, and whether a member has chosen a page. For every member who chose a yearbook as the class page, the class admin also sees the profile and the approved notes and photos of that yearbook (and so the name, message and photos of the friends who wrote those notes), and can print them in the class book. A class admin never sees notes you have not approved, hidden notes, note links, your cover photo or your email address, and cannot change your yearbook.
- **Students** in a class see only their own place in it. They never see the other students' pages, notes or photos, or the list of members.
- **Leaving a class:** if you leave a class, are removed from it, or unlink your page, the class admin loses access to your page at once. Your yearbook is not deleted. Pages the admin already printed or saved outside SMemories are outside our control.
- **Photo links:** if an owner exports a book as text, its photos become links that anyone with the link can open until the owner switches them off.
- **Service providers** that run SMemories for us: Amazon Web Services (hosting, storage and email, region [region]) and Google (only if you sign in with Google). They process data only on our instructions.
- **Authorities**, only when the law requires it.

## 5. Where it is stored
On servers of Amazon Web Services in [region]. [If the region is outside the user's country, state the transfer basis.]

## 6. How long we keep it
- As long as your account or the yearbook exists.
- When you delete a yearbook, its notes and photos are deleted. Backups that still contain them are deleted within [N] days.
- Friends' notes are kept until the owner deletes them or the yearbook.
- Membership of a class is kept until you leave, an admin removes you, or the class is deleted. Deleting a class deletes its members list, invite links and class pages, but never anybody's yearbook. Deleting a yearbook removes it from the class book. An invite link stops working when it expires or an admin revokes it.

## 7. Your rights
You can see, correct and delete your data in the app, and [export it: when available]. You can also ask us at [contact email] to access, correct or delete your data, or to stop using it. Friends can ask the owner of the yearbook or us to remove their note.

## 8. Security
Passwords are stored with a strong one-way hash, connections are encrypted, sign-in attempts are limited, and only approved notes are printed. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at [contact email] if you find a problem.

## 9. Children
Accounts are for people aged 18 or older.

## 10. Changes
When we change this policy we publish a new version with a new date and, if the change matters for you, ask you to accept it the next time you sign in.
