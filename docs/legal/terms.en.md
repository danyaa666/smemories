## 6. Classes

## 7. Note links and photo links
- A note link lets anyone who has it send you a note. Share it only with people you want to hear from. You can turn a link off at any time.
- If you export your book as text, each photo in it becomes a link that anyone who has the link can open without signing in. You can switch all photo links of a book off at any time; links you already shared then stop working (a copy may stay in a cache for a few minutes).
- Friends: the owner of the book you write in may share your note's photos this way. If the owner has put the book in a class, the class admin can also see and print your note once the owner has approved it.
