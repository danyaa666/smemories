Page templates for Memory Book (internal/templates/embed/pagetemplates/memory-book)

These page templates are the project's own authoring: they were made by the SMemories team (task T-172 for the first four, task T-177 for the others) as
placements of registry assets, colours and tilts on our own pages. Like the authored assets (licence "authored", see
web/src/book/assets/licences/authored.txt and docs/assets.md) the team dedicates them to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Nothing is copied from Doory or from any other template source, image or text: no file, picture, wording or layout of another project is in these files. Other
templates and designs were looked at only as inspiration for the kind of look (calm and airy to busy scrapbook), never traced or reused. The files hold no
sentence in any language, no photo and no link.

They use only assets of the registry (web/src/book/assets/registry.json), each under the licence the registry records:
- authored by the team (CC0): shape.burst, shape.heart, shape.star, frame.rounded-border, sticker.doodle-loops, sticker.doodle-sparkle, sticker.doodle-squiggle,
  sticker.doodle-zigzag, sticker.tape-dots, sticker.tape-plain, sticker.tape-stripes;
- CC0 clip art from openclipart.org, licence file in web/src/book/assets/licences/: sticker.cool-sun and sticker.star-face (SunKing2), sticker.flower-badge-yellow
  (yamachem), sticker.gold-star (mazeo), sticker.happy-cloud (Mahua_Sarkar), sticker.paper-plane (monsterbraingames), sticker.red-heart (nerdy).
