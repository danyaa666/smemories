Page templates for Navy Classic (internal/templates/embed/pagetemplates/navy-classic)

These page templates are the project's own authoring: they were made by the SMemories team (task T-172 for the first four, task T-178 for the others) as
placements of registry assets, colours and tilts on our own pages. Like the authored assets (licence "authored", see
web/src/book/assets/licences/authored.txt and docs/assets.md) the team dedicates them to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Nothing is copied from Doory or from any other template source, image or text: no file, picture, wording or layout of another project is in these files. Other
templates and designs were looked at only as inspiration for the kind of look (a formal gold-on-navy book, stately to modern-classic), never traced or reused.
The files hold no sentence in any language, no photo and no link.

They use only assets of the registry (web/src/book/assets/registry.json), all authored by the team (CC0, web/src/book/assets/licences/authored.txt):
shape.burst, shape.circle, shape.heart, shape.rect, shape.star, sticker.doodle-sparkle, sticker.doodle-underline.
